About

I have worked in SAP Basis since 2019 on large landscapes: support packages, transports, system copies, tuning, monitoring, migrations and the cutover weekends that come with them.

Over the last few years my work has moved towards the newer half of the platform: BTP landscape design, identity and single sign-on, Clean Core, SAP Cloud ALM and SAP’s AI services.

I spent six years in Rome, at P&A Solutions and then at Accenture as a team lead. Since 2026 I have lived and worked in Lugano.
